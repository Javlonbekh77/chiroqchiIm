# 1. DESIGN DIRECTION

The website should feel:

* Modern
* Premium
* Trustworthy
* Educational
* Professional
* Slightly futuristic
* Clean and minimal
* Suitable for a high-quality private/modern school

The visual identity must primarily use:

### Main colors:

* Dark deep blue
* Orange / flame orange
* White

Suggested palette:

* Primary Dark Blue: #071A33
* Secondary Blue: #0B2748
* Accent Orange: #FF6A00
* Bright Orange: #FF7A18
* White: #FFFFFF
* Soft White: #F7F9FC
* Muted text: #AAB7C7

The dominant visual color should be dark blue.

* CTA buttons
* Small highlights
* Active navigation states
* Decorative elements

* Hover effects

White should provide contrast and readability.
